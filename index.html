Housewright

One suite for the office, the truck and the job site.

Housewright is our primary product. Desktop runs the office, Companion puts the job on the crew's phones, and Calc does the field math. The office and the phones work from the same records.

Housewright Desktop

For Mac and Windows. Coming soon.

Run the job from the estimate to the final payment on a computer you own. It keeps working with no internet and keeps your numbers in one file that belongs to you.

  • Estimates and proposals your client signs from a link
  • Approved estimates become the budget and schedule of values
  • Gantt schedules for each job and across every job
  • Daily logs, tasks, punch lists and crew time
  • Change orders, pay applications, retainage and lien waivers
  • Job cost and cash flow reports, with a push to QuickBooks Online

Housewright Companion

For iPhone. Coming soon.

The crew's phone. It works with no signal and syncs with the office when it can.

  • Clock in, switch jobs and enter time for a whole crew
  • Tasks and punch lists, with the after photo
  • Daily logs with the weather filled in
  • The current drawing set, with the office's markups
  • Floor plans from a LiDAR room scan
  • Includes Housewright Calc Pro for the whole crew

Housewright Calc

For iPhone and iPad. Coming to the App Store.

The construction calculator, in feet, inches and fractions down to 1/64".

  • 34 trade calculators, from stairs and rafters to decks and concrete
  • A drawing of what you sized on 31 of them, live as you type
  • A starting material list on 30, with waste
  • Code limits flagged where it checks them
  • Speak a measurement, or take one from a Bluetooth Bosch laser
  • Cut sheets with the drawing, inputs and materials on one page
More about Calc

One job, start to finish.

Housewright follows the money the way a job does, so nothing gets typed twice.

  1. Price it

    Build the estimate from your own materials and crew rates. Approve it and it becomes the budget.

  2. Build it

    Schedule the work, tell the subs when things move, and keep a daily log on every job.

  3. Bill it

    Pay applications built from the schedule of values, with change orders and retainage carried through.

  4. Know where you stand

    Job cost against budget and cash flow, from the same numbers you billed from.
