Housewright

Our main product, and the work we spend our days on. Housewright is job management for residential general contractors, built around the way money moves on a job. Three apps share one set of numbers.

Housewright Desktop

Mac and Windows. Coming soon.

Estimates, budgets, the schedule, daily logs, change orders and applications for payment. It runs on your own computer, keeps working with no internet, and keeps your numbers in one file that belongs to you.

The schedule of values for a sample job. Every screenshot here shows a made-up builder.

Housewright Companion

iPhone. Coming soon.

The crew's phone: the time clock, tasks and the punch list, daily logs, photos and floor plans. It works with no signal and syncs with the office when it can.

Housewright Calc

iPhone and iPad. Coming to the App Store.

The construction calculator. Feet, inches and fractions, 34 trade calculators, and drawings of what you sized, live as you type.

Read more about each app at housewright.app.

How we build Housewright

Your records stay with you

The whole book lives in one file on your own computer. No connection, no problem: the desktop keeps working without the internet.

Buy it once, keep it

Housewright is software you own, not rent on your own records. Phone sync costs us a server every month, so it is a separate, optional service.

We can't read your jobs

When phones and the office trade changes, each one is sealed on the device before it leaves. The relay we run passes it along and can't open it.
